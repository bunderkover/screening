Good to know

Other ADHD and autism tests you may hear about

Clinicians use many other questionnaires and interviews. They aren't on this site because they are sold by publishers, need a trained clinician, or need a licence. If one sounds useful, ask your GP or specialist about it.

Free doesn't always mean allowed online. Some tests are free to download for clinics but their owners don't allow them to be copied onto other websites.
