What this is

Quiet Check is a small, free hobby project by underkover. It is vibe-coded: built with AI tools to test what AI models can do, explore their capabilities and have some fun. It is not run by a clinic, a charity or a company.

Screening, not diagnosis

  • The questionnaires here are screening tools. They cannot diagnose ADHD, autism or anything else.
  • Nothing on this site is medical advice. A high score can come from many things, and a low score does not rule anything out.
  • If you are worried about yourself or a child, please speak with your GP (médecin traitant) or a qualified clinician. In an emergency in France call 15 or 112.

The questionnaires

  • Every questionnaire belongs to its authors and publishers, who are credited on each test.
  • Some are free to use. Others are marked with an asterisk: permission from their authors is being sought. Until then they sit behind a password for invited people only. Please do not copy or share them.
  • Taking a test needs a password, given to family and invited people only.

Your answers and privacy

  • Answers are scored in your browser, on your device. They are not stored, sent or saved anywhere, and there are no accounts.
  • If you tick "Remember me", your browser keeps a key for 30 days so you don't need to retype the password. You can clear it any time by clearing site data.
  • We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits. It is anonymous, uses no cookies and does not see your answers.
  • Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google can see that your browser asked for them, as on most websites.

We do not sell or share data, and we do not build profiles of visitors.
